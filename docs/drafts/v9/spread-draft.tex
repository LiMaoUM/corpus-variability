The documents of a repetitive corpus lie close to one another in the embedding space, and those of a diverse corpus lie farther apart. To compare the corpora at equal size, we drew 5{,}000 documents at random from each of the nine corpora, so that the size of a corpus does not change the distances. For each document we measured the cosine distance to its nearest neighbor, the document among the 5{,}000 that is closest to it in meaning. Some of these distances are short and some are long. A distance is short because the documents are close in meaning and gather together, and long because the meaning is spread out. Figure~\ref{fig:spread} shows the distances themselves, because a two dimensional map of the embeddings, such as UMAP, distorts them. The median distance to the nearest neighbor is 0.22 or less in every repetitive corpus and 0.24 or more in every diverse corpus. It ranges from 0.17 in Census citizens to 0.39 in AskDocs. The median distance over all pairs of documents follows the same order, from 0.41 in Census citizens and Census illegal to 0.74 in AskDocs. The distances alone mislead, however, because the same distance marks different content in different corpora. AskDocs looks the most spread out, yet every question has a nearest neighbor within the AskDocs $r_{\mathrm{topic}}$ of 0.60, that is, a neighbor on the same topic. TIFU stories lie closer together, yet the TIFU $r_{\mathrm{topic}}$ is only 0.33. Only 62\% of the stories have a nearest neighbor within that distance. The two groups of corpora differ in where the judged distances fall among the distances to nearest neighbors. In the Trump forum and the Census citizens, Census trump and Census illegal slices, 69\% to 90\% of documents have a nearest neighbor within $r_{\mathrm{same}}$, that is, another document with the same specific content. In Census covid, the fifth repetitive corpus, 41\% do. In the diverse corpora the share is 8\% or less, and it is 0\% in AskDocs and TIFU. A picture of an embedded corpus therefore shows how the corpus is spread only after pairs judged by the model have translated its distances into content.
