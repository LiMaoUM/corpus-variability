\section{Data}
\label{sec:data}

Two synthetic families have a known answer. Gaussian manifolds place 20{,}000 points on a random $d$-dimensional subspace of $\mathbb{R}^{64}$ for $d \in \{2, 4, 8\}$, so the true intrinsic dimension is known. A Zipf topic mixture draws 20{,}000 documents from 200 topic centres with Zipf weights, isotropic Gaussian noise and normalisation to the unit sphere, so the topic of every document is known.

Ten real corpora come from four sources. Three forum corpora were assembled for earlier work on corpus summarization: AskDocs (medical questions from Reddit), Trump (posts about the 2020 election), and TIFU (stories told in the first person on Reddit), 10{,}000 documents each after cleaning and deduplication. They are embedded twice, with GTR-T5-base \citep{ni2022gtr} at three random samples of the source (seeds 42 to 44) and with Qwen3-Embedding-8B \citep{qwen3embedding2025} at seed 42. Five corpora are keyword slices of a 2020 export of tweets about the United States Census: random, citizens, trump, covid and illegal, 7{,}610 to 30{,}136 tweets after URL and mention removal and deduplication of identical text. Two corpora are uniform random samples of 50{,}000 English posts each, drawn from posts of May to December 2024 that we collected from Truth Social and Bluesky through their public interfaces, keeping posts marked English with at least 20 characters after links and mentions are removed, and deduplicated on text, which also removes reposts that copy their original. The Truth Social collection holds the threads under posts about the 2024 candidates, and the Bluesky collection is a broad pool of political posts, so neither is a sample of its platform. All census, Truth Social and Bluesky corpora are embedded with Qwen3-Embedding-8B at 4{,}096 dimensions, truncated at 256 tokens and normalised. Cosine distance, used throughout, gives the same nearest neighbours as the metric $\sqrt{2(1 - \cos)}$.

The posts are public, and the analysis uses only their embeddings and corpus level statistics. No post or account is quoted or identified in the paper. We release the post identifiers of the Truth Social and Bluesky samples, the pair judgments and the code, and no post text.
