\section*{Limitations}

The resolution $r$ is a cosine distance in one encoder's space, and the same $r$ means different things in GTR and in Qwen3 embeddings. The collapse law held under GTR on the forum corpora and under Qwen3 on the census, Truth Social and Bluesky corpora, and $d$ moved with the encoder (13.6 under GTR against 12.6 under Qwen3 on AskDocs), so a user must fix the encoder before choosing $r$ and must report the encoder with $r$.

The exponent $b$ of the stretched exponential is fitted. We have an account of why it is below 1 but no closed form that predicts it from the density, so the formula needs a pilot large enough to fit two coefficients.

The corpora are English social media and forum text from four sources. The Truth Social collection is conditioned on candidate threads, so its $d$ describes that collection and not the platform.

The stability of summaries written from samples, measured as the mean pairwise distance among the summaries of thirty independent samples, falls with $n$ along the coverage curve within each of eight corpora, and across corpora at a fixed $n$ the ordering by coverage does not predict the ordering by stability (Appendix~\ref{app:summaries}). A summary's stability depends on the scale at which the model abstracts, which this paper does not model.
