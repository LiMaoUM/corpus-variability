\section*{Limitations}

The resolution $r$ is a cosine distance in one encoder's space, and the same $r$ means different things in GTR and in Qwen3 embeddings. The collapse law held under both encoders, and $d$ moved with the encoder (13.6 against 12.6 on AskDocs), so a user must fix the encoder before choosing $r$ and must report both.

The exponent $b$ of the stretched exponential is fitted. We have an account of why it is below 1 but no closed form that predicts it from the density, so the formula needs a pilot large enough to fit two coefficients.

The corpora are English social media and forum text from four sources. The Truth Social collection is conditioned on candidate threads, so its $d$ describes that collection and not the platform.

Summary stability was tested and does not follow from coverage across corpora. Within each of eight corpora the instability of summaries written from samples fell with $n$ along the coverage curve, and across corpora at a fixed $n$ the ordering by coverage did not predict the ordering by instability (Appendix~\ref{app:summaries}). A summary's stability depends on the scale at which the model abstracts, which this paper does not model.
